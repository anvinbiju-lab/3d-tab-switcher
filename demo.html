3D Visual Tab Switcher

High-performance dark-mode 3D Visual Tab Switcher with customizable shortcut navigation.

  • No arrow keys required: Press and hold Alt, then tap Q repeatedly to cycle through your tabs!
  • Release to switch: Simply release Alt to immediately activate the selected tab.
  • Reverse cycle: Press Alt + Shift + Q to cycle backward.
  • Custom button: Click the extension icon in the toolbar to change the shortcut button or customize behavior.
